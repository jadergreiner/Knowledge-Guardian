# KGD-012 — Validate finding contract v0.1 and authorize KG-002 shaping

**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Type:** Product and governance  
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The 12-case golden baseline exercised deterministic findings, interpretative findings, human acceptance, revision requested, pre-finding rejection and expected non-findings.

## Decision

1. Validate `knowledge-guardian-finding@0.1.0` for v0.1 shaping and implementation use.
2. Mark `KG-010` complete as the initial manual evaluation baseline.
3. Authorize `KG-002` for shaping only.
4. Keep scanner implementation blocked until KG-002 satisfies the Definition of Ready and a separate delivery commitment is recorded.

## Evidence

- seven positive fixtures structurally valid: `7/7`;
- six findings accepted;
- one finding received `revision_requested` because classification exceeded evidence;
- one authority-less candidate rejected before emission;
- four expected non-findings confirmed: `4/4`;
- no immediate contract gap required schema revision.

## Rationale

The contract represented all curated baseline categories while preserving human authority and false-positive guardrails. Remaining uncertainty concerns implementation, automation and broader reviewer validation rather than the basic finding contract.

## Consequences

- regression tests and executable validation remain delivery requirements;
- real-repository precision and recall remain unvalidated;
- candidate analysis remains internal-only in v0.1;
- KG-002 may define the repository document model but may not trigger scanner delivery automatically.

## Review trigger

Reassess after executable regression tests, the first Knowledge Guardian repository scan, and the first Meu PDI validation run.
